title: "From Field to Code: Spatial Statistics from Scratch"
---

Spatial statistics in Python, from scratch.

This book teaches spatial statistics in Python to readers who have never studied statistics. We start with the foundations: how to look at data, describe it, compare groups, understand probability and decide when a result can be trusted. Then we move on to what makes geographic data special: points, surfaces and areas that resemble their neighbours. Everything runs on real data from Asturias and from the Pajares project on forests, storms and power lines.

The code in every chapter can be copied and run as it is.
